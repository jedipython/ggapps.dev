Trazo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Trazo is designed to generate images on your device. Your prompts, selected photos, and generated images are not uploaded to GG Apps servers for AI processing.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your images, prompts, or generated artwork.
  • We do not collect personal information such as name, email, phone number, or location through the app.
  • We do not use advertising frameworks.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Local Processing And Storage

Text prompts, selected photos, generated images, app preferences, usage counters, and AI model files are processed and stored locally on your device. App-local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall Trazo.

4. Apple Services

Trazo uses Apple services for App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, subscriptions, restore purchases, and optional Photos library access. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

5. Support Requests

If you email us for support, we process the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, device details, screenshots, and app version. Support emails are retained for up to 12 months unless ongoing communication or legal obligations require longer retention.

6. Children's Privacy

Trazo does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app includes safety features intended to reduce inappropriate prompts and outputs.

7. Your Rights

Because Trazo is designed not to collect app usage data or user-generated content, many data subject rights will not apply to app-local data that never leaves your device. For support correspondence, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction by emailing contact@ggapps.dev.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
