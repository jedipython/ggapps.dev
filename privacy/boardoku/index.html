2. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not show ads in Boardoku.
  • We do not use third-party advertising frameworks.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.
  • We do not collect payment card details. Purchases are processed by Apple.

3. Game Data

Boardoku may store game progress, settings, themes, scores, achievements, and feature access locally on your device. App-local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall Boardoku.

4. Online And Competition Features

If you use online features, competitions, leaderboards, or similar social game features, Boardoku may process the information needed to provide those features, such as scores, competition entries, display names or identifiers, timestamps, and anti-abuse signals. These features are used to run the game experience and keep competitions fair.

5. Purchases

Boardoku is free to play and does not include ads. The app offers one optional lifetime purchase for extra features such as Zen mode, competitions, themes, and other premium options. Payments, purchase history, and refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details.

6. Support Requests

If you email us for support, we process the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, device details, screenshots, app version, and purchase or gameplay details relevant to your request. Support emails are retained for up to 12 months unless ongoing communication or legal obligations require longer retention.

7. Children's Privacy

Boardoku does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us through a support request or online feature, contact us and we will review the request.

8. Your Rights

For personal data connected to support correspondence or online features, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction by emailing contact@ggapps.dev.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
